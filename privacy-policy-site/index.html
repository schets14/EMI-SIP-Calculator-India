At a glance: Financial calculations are performed on your device. The app does not require an account and its developer does not operate a server that receives your saved financial snapshots. The app does use Google Mobile Ads, which may process device and ad-related information to provide and measure ads.

1. Overview

EMI SIP Calculator India (“the app”) is a financial-planning calculator published by the app developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). You can use the calculators without registering or connecting a bank account. The results are estimates for planning and are not financial, tax, investment, or legal advice.

This policy covers the Android app and the privacy-policy website hosted for it. It does not replace the privacy terms of third-party services linked from the app.

2. Information handled by the app

Information you enter

Loan amounts, rates, investment assumptions, income and expense figures, savings, saved scenarios, reminders, and calculation history that you enter are processed by the app to provide its features. We do not receive these entries on a developer-operated server.

Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences and app content in local app storage, including language and onboarding status, favorites, recent calculations, saved plans, reminders, and a financial-health snapshot. Calculation history and planning information are stored using the device’s app-private preferences storage. They are not separately encrypted by the app.

Information from advertising services

Google Mobile Ads is integrated to show banner advertising. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad-performance events, subject to Google’s terms and your device or consent settings. We do not control the information these third parties collect.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Information we do not ask for

The app does not ask you to create an account, provide bank login credentials, or connect a bank account. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, or other secrets into calculator fields.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on Google’s systems, applicable requirements, and your device or consent choices. You can manage advertising and privacy controls in your Google account and Android device settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, additional consent choices may be required.

We do not sell your financial calculator entries. Google’s processing of advertising data is governed by Google’s own policies and settings.

4. When information is shared

  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads may process device and ad-related information as described above.
  • When you choose to share: If you export or share a report using your device’s share options, the file or content is passed to the app or service you select. That service’s privacy policy applies.
  • Legal or safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law or to respond to valid legal requests. We do not maintain a developer-side database of your locally stored financial entries.
The privacy-policy website itself is a static site. We have not added analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies to this page. AWS Amplify or related AWS infrastructure may process basic request and security logs as part of hosting; refer to AWS’s Privacy Notice.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

App preferences, history, saved plans, reminders, and financial-health details stay in the app’s local storage until removed or until the app’s data is cleared. You can remove individual items in the relevant app screens where deletion is available. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes local app data.

Android device backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up by the operating system. We do not operate or control Android backup services. Advertising information handled by Google is retained according to Google’s policies and settings.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may apply their own age-related safeguards and requirements.

7. Security

Local app data is kept in app-private storage provided by Android, but the app does not separately encrypt those entries. Protect your device with a screen lock, and avoid entering highly sensitive information. No method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The “Effective date” above shows when it was last revised. Material updates will be reflected on this page; please review it periodically.
